Optional Google Analytics

Public template: Analytics is disabled until the site owner configures a measurement ID. Review and adapt this notice for your own deployment and services.

Google Analytics is loaded only after you choose Allow analytics. Declining does not limit access to the desktop. It measures page views and basic app openings using approved app identifiers. We remove URL queries, fragments and referrers from the events we send.

Google receives technical information such as your browser, device, network address and approximate location, and uses analytics cookies to distinguish visits. Analytics cookies are configured to expire after 60 days. Advertising storage, Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled in our tag. We do not assign a User-ID.

We do not send contact-form contents, chat messages, personal profile content, photos, certificate details or NAS addresses to Google Analytics. If enhanced measurement is enabled in Google Analytics, additional interaction events such as scrolling may also be collected after consent.

You can reopen Analytics preferences below, or from the desktop's hidden system-tray menu, and choose No thanks to withdraw consent. Withdrawal stops this tag and removes its analytics cookies in this browser. It does not delete data already received by Google. Google's privacy policy explains Google's handling of data.

Browser preferences and other services

The site uses browser storage for desktop preferences, your analytics choice and local features such as chat history. Existing Cloudflare hosting and security services process requests independently of optional Google Analytics. This choice controls Google Analytics only.
