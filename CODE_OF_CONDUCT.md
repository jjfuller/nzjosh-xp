# Code of conduct

Reviewed November 18, 2026.

Everyone participating in NZJOSH XP issues, pull requests and project discussions
should be treated with respect, regardless of identity, background or experience.

## Expected conduct

- Be welcoming, constructive and specific when giving feedback.
- Discuss the work, and respect differing opinions and accessibility needs.
- Respect privacy, copyright and the scope of this personal project.
- Do not publish personal photographs, documents, contact information or credentials.

Harassment, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, threats, doxxing, spam,
impersonation and abusive personal attacks are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct concerns, use the Contact Me window at https://nzjosh.com/ and identify
the repository and relevant discussion. Do not post sensitive evidence publicly.
GitHub's Report abuse controls are also available for platform abuse.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, warn
participants, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the
severity and repetition of the conduct. Reports should be handled discreetly;
absolute confidentiality cannot be promised where platform or legal duties apply.

This policy covers the project's GitHub spaces and anyone representing the project.
